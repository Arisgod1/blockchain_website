# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary users are students and prospective new members who want to understand what the DUT Blockchain Team does, whether the team feels credible and active, and how they can join or participate. Secondary users include teachers, alumni, and collaborators who need a clear view of the team's research direction, projects, members, articles, meetings, and contact paths.

## Product Purpose

This website is the public identity and information hub for the Dalian University of Technology Blockchain Team. It should make the team feel research-driven, young, trustworthy, and active by connecting visitors to projects, members, research writing, meeting records, events, and contact information. Success means a new student can quickly understand the team, feel motivated to explore further, and find a concrete next step.

## Brand Personality

Research-minded, youthful, credible. The voice should feel precise and energetic without becoming corporate or over-polished. The site can use technical atmosphere and motion, but the information must stay readable and easy to navigate.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic crypto hype, dark sci-fi clutter, Web3 buzzword marketing, excessive neon, overloaded gradients, card grids that all look the same, and interfaces that feel like a trading platform rather than a university research team. Do not make the public site feel like an enterprise SaaS landing page or a purely administrative dashboard.

## Design Principles

Show the team as active, not abstract: projects, members, meetings, and writing should feel close to the surface.

Make technical depth approachable: use clear structure, short labels, and direct navigation so new students can orient themselves quickly.

Balance exploration with trust: motion and visual effects should suggest curiosity and research energy while preserving legibility and calm.

Use the university-team context with confidence: the interface should feel young and ambitious, but grounded in academic practice.

Turn every page into a next step: each major surface should offer a useful path to learn more, view work, meet people, or contact the team.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion preferences, mobile-first readability, and non-color-only status cues. Motion should enhance the experience without blocking content or making navigation harder.
